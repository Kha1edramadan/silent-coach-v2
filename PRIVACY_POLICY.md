# Silent Coach V2 — Privacy Policy (Draft)

Silent Coach V2 is designed as an offline-first fitness and nutrition app.

## Data stored on the device
The app stores profile data, workout programs, workout logs, body measurements, nutrition logs, saved meals, foods, notification settings, and curated content locally in the app database.

## Network access
Most core features work without a network connection. When the user scans a packaged-food barcode and chooses to look it up online, the barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve public product information. Imported values are treated as unverified until reviewed.

## No hidden credentials
The app must not contain private API keys, passwords, or service credentials inside the APK.

## Backups
The user can export a local JSON backup. The backup contains the app's stored data; users should protect the exported file as they would any personal record.

## Current status
This is a product-preparation document, not legal advice. Before public distribution, replace this draft with the final privacy notice, identify the legal entity/contact responsible for the app, document every third-party service actually enabled, and publish the policy at a stable public URL.
